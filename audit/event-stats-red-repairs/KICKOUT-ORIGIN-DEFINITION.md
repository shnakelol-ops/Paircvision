# Kickout-origin scoring: authoritative definition

**Status:** implemented in `src/stats/chains/kickout-phase.ts` on branch `claude/event-stats-intelligence-repairs`, draft PR #376, stacked on PR #374. It is **not merged** and awaits approval.

## The definition

A **kickout-origin (puckout-origin) score** is a score by the team that won the kickout, in the possession that kickout started. This is the existing product specification: "Restart-origin counts every score in a possession that began with a kickout, including frees won during it" (`restartAttributionFootnote`). What the specification never said was when that possession ends. It now does.

**The possession ends at the first of:**

1. A score by that team. That score is the kickout-origin score.
2. A wide or missed free by that team.
3. **A change of possession:**
   - any turnover, whichever team it is recorded under;
   - any attempt (score, wide or shot) by the other team, who can only shoot if they had the ball;
   - a free, 45/65 or sideline awarded to the other team.
4. The next kickout or puckout.
5. 90 s with none of the above.

**These do not end it:**

- A free, 45/65 or sideline awarded to the team in possession. Scores from it are kickout-origin; placed-ball scores are still filed under Placed balls by the scoring ledger, and the existing footnote reports how many ("Origin possessions include N placed frees…").
- A blocked, saved, short or post shot by that team. The ball may be recovered, and the next recorded event decides.

**Which side has the ball after an award.** After a `*_WON` event, it is `teamSide`; after a `*_CONCEDED` event, it is the other team. This applies to frees, 45/65s and sidelines from Event Stats, Match Stats and Rapid Capture.

## Where it applies

| Engine | Uses the definition for | Difference by design |
|---|---|---|
| Chain engine: kickout dataset (`chain-engine.ts`) | Restart Analysis and Restart Chain origin rows, "Restarts Won → Scores", restart rows of the scoring ledger, Opposition Origin, Turnover Chain insights, coaching prompts | None. This **is** kickout-origin scoring. |
| Possession-outcomes engine (`possession-outcomes-engine.ts`) | Kickout family: Quick Review, outcome cards, Intelligence Summary | It measures the *immediate* outcome. As documented for that engine, a free won by the team in possession starts a new phase (its "frees" family). The change-of-possession rule is identical. |

Both engines call one function, `kickoutPhaseStep()`, so they cannot disagree about a change of possession. Their only remaining difference is a free won by the team in possession. A test (`kickout-phase.test.ts`) asserts this on a real match.

## Why this definition

**It keeps the written specification.** Frees won during the possession remain kickout-origin, as the product already states, and the bridge footnote stays true.

**It fixes I-3 precisely.** The old rule ("the winner's first score before the next kickout") ignored possession changes completely.

**It is validated against independent ground truth.** The audit simulator knows the real origin of every score. Rebuilding the possession root of each score from it (the last event at which the ball changed hands) gives:

| Rule | Kickout-origin scores credited | Correct | Credited although the ball had changed hands | Missed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Previous rule (production and the original reference) | 74 | 60 | **14** | 0 |
| **Authoritative definition** | 59 | 59 | **0** | 1* |

\* A-130: a scripted fixture extra (an opposition penalty, A-X02) is inserted in the middle of a Ballyboden possession. This cannot happen in a real match.

Evidence: `scripts/diagnostics/kickout-origin-rules.mjs` → `results/kickout-origin-rules-90s.json`.

## Alternative considered and not chosen

**"Kickout phase":** end the origin at **any** free, 45/65 or sideline, even one won by the same team. It matches the simulator's stoppage-based origin labels exactly (40/40). It would have made the two engines identical, but it contradicts the written specification ("including frees won during it") and would have changed far more coach-facing figures. For example, Ballylanders' real-match kickout-origin rate would be 2/14 instead of 4/14.

If you prefer that definition, it is a one-line change in `kickoutPhaseStep()`: `RESTART_RETAINED` → end. It would also need a decision on the bridge footnote.

## The independent reference

`expected/` (the original ledgers) is unchanged and byte-identical to the audit commit `a8581a5`. It still encodes the previous rule, so checks of kickout-origin figures fail against it on the Intelligence branch by design.

**The authoritative expectations** are in `expected-authoritative/`, generated by `scripts/reference-kickout-phase.mjs`.

**Independence:**
- The script imports the unchanged `reference.mjs` and no `src/` code.
- It first proves that `reference.mjs` still reproduces `expected/` exactly.
- It then recomputes only the fields that depend on this rule: `chains.*.restart*`, `chains.*.origins`, `origins` and `possession.restartWon/Lost`.

**Validation** (`expected-authoritative/VALIDATION.json`) against possession roots from simulator truth:

| Ledger | Credited | Correct | Wrong | Missed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 21 | 21 | 0 | 1 (the A-130 artifact) |
| B live | 17 | 17 | 0 | 0 |
| B after review edits | 18 | 17 | 1 | 0 |
| C | 21 | 21 | 0 | 0 |

The one "wrong" credit in B after review edits is B-266. It was tagged as a wide and edited to a score, so the simulator truth still records the original wide. After the edit, the score genuinely belongs to B-265's kickout possession.

## Open decision (wording only, not made)

The Intelligence Summary and outcome cards print the possession engine's *immediate* kickout outcome in sentences such as "Ballyboden scored from 6 of 16 own Kickouts retained". The Restart Analysis "Won → Score (origin)" row prints kickout-origin scoring. They now differ only when a team scored from a free it won during the possession.

Relabelling either sentence ("immediately", or "origin") would make the difference explicit. That is a report-copy change, so it is left for your approval.
